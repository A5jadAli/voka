# Google Play store listing (ASO)

Copy-ready text for Play Console > Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing. Google Play has no hidden keyword field: it ranks on the **title** (strongest), the **short description** and the **full description**, plus installs, ratings and retention. Keywords are woven in naturally; repeating them does not help and can break Play's metadata policy.

Limits: title 30, short description 80, full description 4000 characters. `node scripts/check-store-listing.mjs` verifies every field below.

Graphics in this folder: `icon-512.png` (hi-res icon) and `feature-graphic.png` (1024 × 500). Screenshots: add 4 to 8 real phone screenshots (portrait, 1080 × 1920 or larger). Suggested order: live conversation, speaking mock test, German lesson, answer feedback, progress. Screenshots must show the real app.

Target keywords (English): speak English, English speaking practice, IELTS speaking practice, learn German, German for beginners, German A1 A2 B1, AI tutor, conversation practice, pronunciation, vocabulary.

Policy guardrails: no "best", "#1", "free" or prices in the title; no claims of certificates, official band scores or guaranteed levels; state that Vokeno is independent of IELTS.
