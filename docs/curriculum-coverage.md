# Curriculum coverage, Voka 1.5 (draft)

These are original, scaffolded practice activities. A level label describes intended task difficulty, not validated course equivalence or a learner's attained level. See [curriculum-review-2026-09.md](curriculum-review-2026-09.md) for the review behind this version.

## Guided lessons (one engine, both languages)

Every guided lesson has: phrases with meaning and usage notes, normal and slow device audio, a usage notice, a sound-and-rhythm tip, 3–4 checks (most include a listen-first item), constrained writing with typo-aware feedback, and a self-reported speaking task. Option order is shuffled stably so answers cannot be guessed by position.

| Live speaking     | 6 units per language, plus task-card speaking mocks (IELTS Parts 2–3; Goethe B1 Teil 1 and 2) with preparation timer, notes and an examiner who listens without interrupting. All guided activities work without an account or microphone. Text content works offline. Audio depends on an installed device voice. Speaking checkboxes are self-reports, not assessed pronunciation. Live voice requires network access, consent and a backend session allowance.

| Spaced review | Every phrase in a finished lesson becomes a review card (1, 3, 7, 16, 35, 90 days). Items rotate from recognition to listening to spoken recall. Missed items come back the same session and the next day |

## Honest scope

- Reaching B1 typically takes several hundred hours of study and use. The German path gives structured coverage of the core A1–B1 grammar and situations, but it is not enough on its own; learners also need volume (listening and reading), spaced review and regular live conversation.
- The placement check is short and unvalidated. It suggests a starting lesson; it is not a CEFR result.
- IELTS lessons are preparatory. There are no full-length timed mocks and no band scores.

## Remaining educational validation

- Independent German and English educator and native-speaker review of the new text and audio prompts.
- Real zero-German learner sessions, including learners with limited English.
- A larger unseen item bank, spaced retrieval of lesson items and calibration of the placement check against an external measure.
- Validated writing and voice rubrics before presenting any scores.
- Full-length, timed IELTS simulations before describing this as a complete IELTS course.

IELTS task formats were checked against the official [Academic](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing) and [General Training](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-general-training-test/ielts-general-training-format-writing) writing pages and the public [speaking band descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf).
