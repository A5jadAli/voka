# Curriculum coverage, Voka 1.4

These are original, scaffolded practice activities. A level label describes intended task difficulty, not validated course equivalence or a learner's attained level.

| Target           | Guided lessons                                                                          | Language focus                                                                                    | Skills exercised                                                                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First words / A1 | Greetings, names, asking for repetition, numbers/prices, café orders, directions/trains | Polite chunks, Sie/du, negation, basic questions, selected articles and numbers                   | Translated reading, device listening, constrained writing, optional oral rehearsal  |
| A2               | Appointments, housing, a past day, symptoms/clarification                               | Time expressions, verb-second order, Perfekt with haben/sein, duration with seit, polite requests | Short contextual reading and comprehension plus the guided four-skill loop          |
| B1               | Opinions, workplace problems, complaints                                                | Reasons/contrast with weil/obwohl, dass clauses, modals, connected explanations and register      | Context reading, evidence checks, sentence production and extended speaking prompts |
| English          | Library notice, study findings, workshop rules                                          | Main idea, detail, inference limits, conditions                                                   | Reading with nine questions, correction and persisted completion                    |
| English writing  | Chart, request letter, opinion                                                          | Overview/comparison, audience/tone, supported arguments                                           | Persistent drafts, saved submissions, examples and rule-based revision prompts      |
| Optional IELTS   | Academic or General Training goal, four-skill guide                                     | Goal-specific writing plus reading, listening and adaptive live conversation                      | Short preparatory practice, not a complete or timed mock exam                       |

All German guided activities work without an account or microphone. Text content works offline in the APK. Audio depends on an installed German device voice. Speaking checkboxes are self-reports, not assessed pronunciation. Live voice requires network access, consent and a backend session allowance.

## Remaining educational validation

- Independent educator/native-speaker review of German text, progression and pronunciation guidance.
- Real zero-German learner sessions, including people with limited English support language.
- Unseen assessments, a wider item bank, adaptive retrieval practice and a complete CEFR outcome map.
- Broader grammar, vocabulary, sustained reading/listening and transfer tasks before any A2/B1 proficiency promise.
- Validated writing/voice rubrics before presenting scores. Current transcript estimates cannot measure pronunciation.
- Full-length and varied exam simulations before describing this as a complete IELTS preparation course.

IELTS task distinctions and full-task word counts were checked against the official [Academic writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing) and [General Training writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-general-training-test/ielts-general-training-format-writing). Voka's shorter exercises are explicitly labelled preparatory, not full exam responses. Voka is not affiliated with IELTS or Goethe-Institut.
